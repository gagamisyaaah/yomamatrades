# Dragon C as a portfolio — $100,000, 643 tickers, 2016-09-19 → 2026-09-18

One account, traded by the rules below. The signal is read at a day's close, the position is bought at the next morning's open, and it is sold at the close of the first day the hole flips to −1. Size: a 1-ATR move is worth 1.0% of the account (never more than 20% of the account in one name). At most 10 names at once; extra signals are simply not taken, biggest-ATR first. No leverage: the open positions never add up to more than the account. 0.05% commission each way. Profits compound.

Tiers: **C** = hole broke up + whales ≥ 50 + TE up + not overheated + Mountain ≥ 3 + above gold. **D** = C, and the hole broke up within the last 3 bars (fresh). **E** = D, and at least 6 of the 7 Dragon panels are bullish. **F** = C, only on names whose ATR is ≥ 5 % of price. **G** = C with the market guard: no entries while SPY is below its 200-day line, exit when it crosses below.

## Caveats

- **Read the random row first.** Buying these names on random days already compounds at a high rate with a deep drawdown, because the universe is today's watch-list of names that have already run (crypto miners, quantum, AI). A big part of every tier's return is the universe, not the signal; the signal's own worth is the gap between its row and the random row, and that gap is modest next to the size of the swings.
- The trade list comes from `mine.evaluate.simulate`: entries on the rising edge of the rule, one ledger row per signal. A name can re-signal while it is still held (the rule flickers inside one hole cycle); the account takes one position per name, so those repeats are counted as *skipped: held*, not as slots.
- Sizing divides by the signal bar's ATR; bars with ATR = 0 (bad or flat data) are skipped and counted.
- Exits are the ledger's flip exit only — no stop-loss. A position that never flips is closed at the last bar in the data, at that close.
- Daily equity marks open positions at each day's close; a name that is halted on a day carries its last close forward.
- Names that listed late (e.g. CRWV 2025, RDDT 2024) contribute nothing before their first bar; the early years are traded on fewer names than the later ones.
- Yahoo/Nasdaq daily bars, adjusted as the source delivers them; no survivorship correction — the universe is the founder's current watch-list.
